About Gadiya Time System:

• Total 60 Gadiya in 24 hours (30 day + 30 night)

• Each Gadiya = 24 minutes

• Day starts at sunrise (6:00 AM), Night starts at sunset (6:00 PM)

• Day Gadiya: 1-30, Night Gadiya: 1-30
