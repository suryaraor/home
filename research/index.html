Adaptive Cognitive Data Fabric System

Inventors: Surya Rao Rayarao, Santhosh Kumar Pendyala, Vijay Kumar Adari, and others

Filed: 2025 | Patent Number: PTO/AIA/14 OMB 0651-0032

Patent Overview

The Adaptive Cognitive Data Fabric (ACDF) patent introduces an innovative approach to the autonomous management of complex data ecosystems. It leverages quantum-resistant security, AI-driven optimization, and blockchain-based federated governance to ensure data integrity, scalability, and compliance across diverse environments.

Key Features

  • Quantum-Resistant Data Collector: Secure, multi-protocol data ingestion using post-quantum cryptographic safeguards.
  • Self-Healing Stream Reconciliation: Ensures synchronization of heterogeneous data streams through Byzantine fault tolerance.
  • Adaptive Pipeline Orchestrator: AI-based dynamic optimization of data workflows with reinforcement learning algorithms.
  • Cognitive Model Retrainer: Uses continual learning techniques to prevent concept drift and maintain model accuracy.
  • Federated Metadata Manager: Blockchain-based immutable provenance tracking to ensure compliance and governance transparency.

Importance of the Patent

This patent addresses a critical need for secure, adaptable, and efficient data management frameworks capable of handling multi-source, multi-modal data across distributed environments. As data volumes continue to grow, traditional systems are ill-equipped to manage such vast and varied datasets, often resulting in inefficiencies and security vulnerabilities.

This innovation provides a scalable, secure, and intelligent framework for autonomous data governance, ensuring compliance with evolving regulations without compromising performance. Its implications span across industries such as healthcare, finance, and IoT, where data integrity, real-time processing, and regulatory adherence are paramount.
